# Guild and delegated lifecycle completion

This update closes the reviewed Standard Guild Hall and delegated-crate milestone. It strengthens existing housing operations; no new opcode, packet layout or schema migration is introduced.

## Furnished relocation

Direct house return and contained-house relocation share a structural preflight under the current inventory/session transaction. Existing creator, current guild/ACL and destination authorization still apply. The preflight rejects occupied rooms, pending publication, stale profiles, incomplete or conflicting room scopes, and orphaned storage, vault or placed-crate records. Independently enumerated native-room reservations and container provenance prevent a shortened presentation list from hiding missing contents. Legitimate carried crates retaining room provenance remain supported after typed custody/member validation.

A safely carried house keeps its permanent interior and historical parent. Offline retirement now distinguishes that exact detached typed binding from an unaccounted active building. It checks the creator-owned inventory token, permanent room/catalogue/instance, offline and pending state, and the same complete structural census. It leaves the room and furnishings intact; unknown children and corrupt bindings still defer retirement.

## Return history after later moves

A completed whole-item return records its historical destination generation. Picking up its generated moving crate or subsequently releasing that destination must not invalidate the original source settlement merely because the crate's current parent changed.

The verifier now checks current typed crate custody independently from the immutable return destination. A released destination requires its exact authenticated manual retirement record, or its exact automatic inactivity record plus a recursively verified full conservation seal. Missing/corrupt markers, receipts, raw custody, finance or seals still refuse the operation. Recursion is cycle checked and bounded. Completed history does not acquire a new present-day guild-membership requirement. Older automatic releases without full conservation seals are not promoted to accepted proof.
