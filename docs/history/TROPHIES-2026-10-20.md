# Verified trophy profile, October 20, 2026

This update makes the supported trophy profile explicit and removes guessed
minimum levels. It introduces no opcode, packet layout or client-file change.
The three definitions below combine published values and retained later item
collector associations. They are reconstructed content, not an authenticated
May 2013 Live catalogue.

| Benefit | Physical item | Minimum level | Favor per cycle | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal Trophy of the Giant | 68730 | 95 | 75 | +400 HP, mana and endurance |
| Personal Trophy of the Landing | 68727 | 95 | 250 | +45 AC, +20 heroic stamina |
| Guild Trophy of Hardened Steel | Empowered Trophy of Hardened Steel, 66750 | 90 | 225 | +250 HP, +50 AC, +20 heroic strength |

The profile uses dedicated hidden stat items, not the physical items' equipment
statistics. Activation still requires a valid placed source, eligible recipient,
selection, permissions and sufficient favor. Original RoF2 help supports a
ten-minute cycle; pause, withdrawal and offline renewal are the existing emulator
paid-time policies rather than newly asserted historical rules.

## Content and runtime changes

- Fresh catalogue SQL contains these three definitions. The guarded correction
  withdraws the ten earlier final-tradeskill seeds whose minimum level was an
  explicit level-1 default. It accepts exact old seeds or wholly absent triplets,
  rejects custom/partial rows and affected selections, and rolls back on error.
  It preserves physical items, evolution, placements, favor and charge history.
- Landing and Giant share original-client model IT11223. The optional placement
  repair combines its pinned original geometry with retained later collector
  placement metadata. It requires exact item identities and empty or already
  correct fields. Runtime checks require the reviewed mask, scales, upkeep and
  item cap; unknown restriction flags remain unchanged.
- Yard Add now enforces positive per-item estate limits from current metadata,
  including the personal trophies' maximum of one per item per estate. It counts
  stowed yard items, excludes typed room/crate members, locks the physical native
  estate and decodes canonical custody rows. Moves and reads do not count the
  existing source as a new Add. Interior Add already enforces its own realm cap.
- Guild billing takes identity, account, lease and pending-house locks before
  guild state in a consistent order. Shared-account recipients use separate
  sorted character/account phases, avoiding the zone/billing lock-order cycle.

## Native acceptance and limits

The original client placed the guild trophy, selected it, opted a member in and
activated it with a 225-favor debit. Disabling removed the applied bonuses
(HP 4046 to 3796; strength 158 to 138). Its state survived a cold server restart;
resuming paid time caused no additional debit. Picking up the placed source
invalidated the benefit. This establishes the representative guild workflow,
not every roster, rank or multi-member billing combination.

The original client completed a representative personal cycle with Trophy of
the Giant on an actual personal plot, using the reviewed mask12 and scales0.1–2.5.
It selected the level95, 75-favor benefit; the UI Activate button was disabled
with zero favor. This is a client UI observation, not a server insufficient-favor
packet check. Following administrative offline funding and relog, both placement
and selection persisted. Native activation charged exactly75 favor (1000 to925)
and increased HP from3796 to4196 and endurance from2967 to3367, matching +400.
Disabling restored both baseline values. Paused paid time survived logout/relog;
resuming retained the925 favor balance and the original single75-favor charge.
Native pickup from nearby returned the physical trophy to inventory, cleared
selection, activation and remaining paid time, and restored the baseline stats.
An out-of-range pickup was refused before approaching the source.

Landing uses the same reviewed original IT11223 model and placement class, with
its separate researched stat definition. It has not received an individual
native gameplay check; representative shared-model coverage is distinct from
accepting every item independently.

Evolving tradeskill trophy placement and housing benefits remain disabled.
Reviewed geometry and known 15% effects/zero cost do not establish unknown
minimum levels or preserve the separate evolving identity. See the
[evolving custody contract](../../overlay/common/housing/evolving_trophy_custody.md).
Other incomplete or conflicting catalogue entries remain excluded.

## Deployment

Follow the [installation sequence](../INSTALL.md#verified-trophy-profile-october-20).
Stop world and every zone before applying content changes and regenerating the
**active shared-item prefix**, then restart with matching binaries. A successful
`#hotfix` alone is insufficient: this upstream path can populate items through
`content_db`, while normal zone startup attaches the item dataset through
`database`. Verify the actual cache source and active prefix, not just SQL rows
or a successful hotfix message.
