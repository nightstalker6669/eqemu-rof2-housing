# In-game screenshots

These screenshots were captured from the original May 10, 2013 RoF2 client connected to the local housing server. The scenes were arranged for this project; they are not screenshots of Daybreak Live and do not establish every gameplay behavior or every historical data value.

The Palatial scene shows decorations distributed around the permitted yard, rather than preset furnishing sockets. Evantil’s Abode and Hermit’s Hideaway are personal-house variants. The interior scene shows placed furniture and reviewed NPC service contracts.
